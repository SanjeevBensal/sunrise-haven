The haven

A house built around a view, not beside it.

Sunrise Haven sits on a quiet ridge outside the city center, where Baguio's pine belt opens onto a wide eastern horizon. We designed the property around a single fact: the sunrise here is worth waking up for.

Every room faces the same direction. Every window is a little larger than it needs to be. What's left is a small, unhurried retreat — eight rooms, a shared garden, and a view that changes by the hour.
